KLING 3.0 OMNI - DIALOGUE FORMAT THAT WORKS (perfect lip sync, confirmed on clip 4)
Settings: first frame = last frame of previous clip, 15 s, 16:9, Multi-shot OFF, Audio ON.
Elements: @littlerhino @mamarhino + location (max 3).

Prompt pattern:
- "Continue exactly from the first frame." + style + who is where.
- Split into time blocks: "0-4 s: ...", "4-9 s: ...".
- In every block say whose mouth moves and that the others stay CLOSED.
- Dialogue: "speaks slowly in a soft, warm, gentle mother's voice: "..."" /
            "answers in his small, high, squeaky voice: "...""  (never say "boy" or "child")
- End with: "Lip sync rules: a mouth moves ONLY when that character is speaking a line,
  exactly matching the words and timing. No mouth movement at any other time.
  No silent lip movement." + Sound list + "No music. No text."

STRONGER NO-EXTRA-LIP RULES (added after clips 6-9 still had small extra mouth movement)
- In every silent block write: "mouths closed in a gentle closed-mouth smile, lips still".
- Do NOT write "giggling", "laughing", "chattering" in silent blocks (they make Kling open mouths).
  Use "smiling with mouth closed", "happy eyes", "shoulders shake with a silent laugh".
- Put the spoken line as ONE short sentence, and say "then the mouth closes and stays closed".
- Kling NEGATIVE PROMPT field: talking, mouth moving, lip movement, chewing, babbling,
  open mouth, mouth flapping, mumbling, extra speech, singing
