VEYRA - PROMPTS 19 AND 20 (V2: multi-shot + physics, cuts and environment explained per shot)

What is new in V2:
- Each 30-second video is ONE video built from 8 short shots (3-4 seconds each) joined by hard cuts. The model only has to hold physics for a few seconds at a time.
- Every move is explained physically: START position -> FORCE (what drives it) -> MOTION & CONTACT (direction, what stays touching what) -> WEIGHT & REACTION (how bodies, cloth, dust react) -> END position.
- Every cut is explained: CUT IN (how the shot picks up from the previous one) and CUT OUT (the exact moment it hands over to the next).
- Every shot has its own ENVIRONMENT (which part of the location, light sources and direction, surfaces, atmosphere) and BACKGROUND MOTION (everything else that moves, and how).
- One action per shot. Screen direction is locked for the whole video.
- No frame images are used. The same full set of 21 main reference images (all CHAR_, GUEST_, ENEMY_ and ENV_ files) is uploaded with every prompt. Each prompt names which of them this video uses; the rest are ignored.
