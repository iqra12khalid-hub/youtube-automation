# VEYRA — Script v2

~20 minutes · 40 clips × 30 seconds · follows `VEYRA_V2_STORY_OUTLINE.md`

Format per clip: **setting**, **who is in it**, **what happens** (the story beats, in order), **dialogue** (short lines, timing is set later in the prompts), and **link** to the previous clip (C = continues from its last frame, CUT = new scene).
Characters are named only by their reference sheets. No ages anywhere.

---

# ACT 1 — THE FALL

## 1 · IT COMES
**Setting:** the sky above Solace at golden dusk (ENV_06), then the city (ENV_01). **Who:** no characters. **Link:** CUT (opening).
- Black screen. A single amber pulse, like a heartbeat, three times.
- Golden clouds under the ringed gas giant and the twin suns. Three white-and-gold Solace fighters glide past.
- The clouds split open. The black Khorr warship (ENV_05b) pushes through, nose first, huge and silent.
- Street level in Solace: its shadow slides across the bone-white towers. The glowing Vertebra Spine Tower dims. Rows of vents under the warship open and glow red.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 2 · FIRST STRIKE
**Setting:** Solace as the attack begins (ENV_02a → ENV_02b). **Who:** no characters. **Link:** C.
- The red vents fire. Thick red beams hit three towers; their tops burst into fire and falling debris.
- A beam cuts a road bridge in two; cars brake at the edge.
- Solace fighters launch from a tower platform and climb toward swarms of black Khorr strike craft pouring out of the warship.
- Wide: the city now burning under the black ship, smoke rising into the gold sky.
**Dialogue:** (radio, calm female commander) "All Solace wings — scramble. Hostile over the Spine."

## 3 · THE CALL
**Setting:** a rocky terrace at the edge of the burning city (ENV_02a). **Who:** Teo. **Link:** CUT.
- Teo stumbles along a stone path, ash falling, and stops to look back at the fires.
- His right forearm: the white-and-amber gauntlet lights up by itself, faint amber lines running up his arm.
- He grips his wrist, confused, breathing hard.
- He looks up. The warship's prow slides overhead and its shadow swallows the terrace in red light.
**Dialogue:** Teo (whisper) "It's calling me."

## 4 · THE KEY
**Setting:** the warship's bridge (ENV_05c). **Who:** Commander, one Hunter (Elite). **Link:** CUT.
- The Commander sits on the high throne, back to us, watching the burning city through the vast window.
- A red hologram of Solace rises; many small amber dots light up.
- One dot burns brighter than all the others. Red target rings lock onto it.
- The Commander raises one armoured hand and points. The kneeling Hunter rises and walks out.
**Dialogue:** Commander (deep, slow) "There. The key." … "Bring it to me." Hunter (cold whisper) "It will be done."

## 5 · THE HUNTERS FALL
**Setting:** under the warship (ENV_05b), then the sky above the burning city (ENV_02b). **Who:** three Hunters. **Link:** CUT.
- A hatch in the warship's belly slides open; red light spills into the smoke.
- Three Hunters step off and drop, capes whipping, spreading into a wedge.
- Beside one Hunter as it dives between smoke columns and towers.
- Its faceless helmet, three red eyes flaring, the fires rushing up below.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 6 · RUN
**Setting:** a long elevated bridge walkway in the burning city (ENV_02b). **Who:** Teo, three Hunters. **Link:** CUT.
- Wide side view: Teo runs along the walkway, left to right, past stalled cars.
- Far behind him, the three Hunters slam down onto the bridge in a burst of dust.
- The Hunters rise together, glaives igniting, and start running after him.
- Teo glances back, sees them, and runs harder toward the far end.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 7 · SURROUNDED
**Setting:** the same bridge (ENV_02b). **Who:** Teo, Khorr soldiers. **Link:** C.
- A black dropship roars low over the bridge ahead of Teo.
- Soldiers drop from it and land in a line, blocking the end of the bridge, carbines up.
- Teo stops. He raises the gauntlet; it sputters and dies.
- The soldiers step forward together, red aiming lights crossing the air in front of them. A shadow flickers across them from above.
**Dialogue:** Soldier (flat, metallic) "Target located. Take it intact."

## 8 · NYRA
**Setting:** the same bridge (ENV_02b). **Who:** Nyra, Teo, soldiers. **Link:** C.
- High on a burning tower, Nyra stands against the fire, braids and fur mantle blowing — then steps off.
- She drops through the smoke and lands on one knee between Teo and the soldiers; the road cracks in a ring around her.
- She rises and draws both swords from her back; blue-white lightning runs along the blades.
- The soldiers adjust their aim to her. She settles into her stance.
**Dialogue:** Nyra (low, calm) "Get behind me."

## 9 · BLADES  🔴
**Setting:** the same bridge (ENV_02b). **Who:** Nyra, one Hunter. **Link:** C.
- The soldiers fire; Nyra deflects the bolts in bursts of blue sparks and cuts through their carbines. They fall back.
- A Hunter lands behind her and charges. One-on-one: lightning swords against the red glaive, heavy exchanges.
- The Hunter drives her back step by step, her boots scraping the road.
- Blades locked, her face close to its three red eyes. She shoves it back and resets her stance.
**Dialogue:** Hunter (cold) "You protect what you don't understand." Nyra "I understand enough."

## 10 · THE GAUNTLET
**Setting:** the same bridge (ENV_02b). **Who:** Teo, Nyra, Hunter. **Link:** C.
- The Hunter raises its glaive over Nyra.
- Behind her, Teo thrusts his right arm forward; the gauntlet's core flares blinding amber.
- An amber shockwave throws the Hunter across the bridge into a wrecked car. The blast cracks the road under Nyra and Teo.
- The section tilts. Nyra grabs Teo's hand and they slide down into the dark opening below.
**Dialogue:** Teo (shocked) "I didn't mean to—" Nyra "Hold on!"

# ACT 2 — BELOW

## 11 · BELOW
**Setting:** the underground market (ENV_03). **Who:** Nyra, Teo. **Link:** CUT.
- From below: Nyra and Teo crash through a cloth stall canopy and roll out onto wet stone.
- Wide: the market in panic — people flee into side tunnels, lanterns swing, dust falls from the hole above.
- Nyra pulls Teo up.
- They look up at the glowing hole. Red lights appear at its edge.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "Up. Move."

## 12 · WHO ARE YOU
**Setting:** a small stone chamber under the market (ENV_03). **Who:** Nyra, Teo. **Link:** CUT.
- Nyra and Teo stumble in and slide down against the wall, coughing, one teal lamp flickering.
- They catch their breath and look at each other for the first time.
- A red scanner beam sweeps across a ceiling grate above them; heavy footsteps pass.
- Silence. The footsteps fade. Nyra almost smiles.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "Who are you?" Teo "…Teo." Nyra "Well, Teo — everyone up there wants you."

## 13 · THE ELDER
**Setting:** a quiet side passage of the market (ENV_03). **Who:** Oris, Nyra, Teo. **Link:** CUT.
- A heavy door creaks open; Oris stands there with his bone staff and waves them in.
- Inside, he looks at Teo's gauntlet for a long moment.
- He points his staff down a passage lined with amber lamps.
- Nyra nods; they go. Oris closes the door behind them and stays.
**Dialogue:** Oris (old, gravelly) "They'll tear the city apart for that." … "Find Kael. Follow the amber lamps."

## 14 · RED EYES
**Setting:** a steam-filled tunnel (ENV_03). **Who:** one Hunter, Nyra, Teo. **Link:** CUT.
- A burst pipe fills the tunnel with white steam. Three red eyes glow inside it.
- The Hunter charges out at Nyra; she blocks and is pushed back against the wall.
- Nyra shouts; Teo aims the gauntlet at the cracked ceiling above the Hunter.
- The ceiling comes down between them. Red eyes glow through a gap in the rubble, then fade.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "Teo — the ceiling!"

## 15 · THE SILO
**Setting:** Kael's hidden hangar (ENV_08) with the Rust Moth (ENV_09). **Who:** Kael, Dax. **Link:** CUT.
- Kael lies under the Rust Moth's nose, fixing something; Dax carries a crate across the hangar.
- A knock at the small side door. Both freeze. Kael draws his revolver.
- The door opens: Nyra and Teo, dusty and exhausted.
- Kael lowers the revolver slightly. Dax looks at Kael.
**Dialogue:** Dax "Friends of yours?" Kael "Not yet."

## 16 · RESONANCE
**Setting:** the hangar (ENV_08). **Who:** Teo, Kael (Nyra and Dax watching). **Link:** C.
- Nyra steps in front of Teo, hand on a sword hilt.
- Teo steps out; his gauntlet starts to glow by itself — and at the same moment the amber lines on Kael's arm light up.
- The two glows pulse in the same rhythm; tools on the wall start to rattle.
- Kael pulls his arm back; the glow fades. He turns to Dax.
**Dialogue:** Kael (quiet) "Where did you get that?" Teo "It found me." Kael "Dax. Fire up the Moth."

## 17 · THEY'RE HERE
**Setting:** the hangar (ENV_08). **Who:** Dax, soldiers (Kael, Nyra, Teo in background). **Link:** C.
- A red-hot line starts tracing across the main blast door from outside; molten metal drips.
- Dax drags crates into a barricade and racks his shotgun.
- Heavy impacts dent the door inward, again and again.
- The door section bursts in; soldiers appear in the smoke.
**Dialogue:** Kael "Get him on the ship!"

## 18 · HOLD THE DOOR  🔴
**Setting:** the hangar (ENV_08). **Who:** Dax, Kael, Nyra, soldiers. **Link:** C.
- Static wide: heroes on the right behind crates, soldiers entering from the left through smoke.
- The soldiers advance firing; crates splinter. Dax fires back; a soldier is knocked off his feet.
- Nyra deflects bolts at the ramp; Kael fires from the landing gear.
- Dax backs up the ramp last, still firing, as it starts to close.
**Dialogue:** Dax "Go! I'm right behind you!"

## 19 · COLD START
**Setting:** the Rust Moth cockpit (ENV_04). **Who:** Kael, Teo. **Link:** C.
- Kael drops into the pilot seat and flips switches; amber dials flicker on; engines whine up.
- Bolts spark against the cockpit glass; a crack spreads.
- Teo straps in beside him, gripping the seat.
- Kael pulls a lever; far above, the silo's round hatch grinds open to the burning sky.
**Dialogue:** Kael "Come on, old girl."

## 20 · LIFT OFF
**Setting:** the silo (ENV_08), then the sky above the burning city (ENV_02b). **Who:** no characters visible (the Rust Moth, ENV_09). **Link:** C.
- The Rust Moth's thrusters ignite; dust and crates blast across the hangar floor.
- It lifts, rising straight up the silo shaft, hull scraping sparks off the walls.
- It bursts out of the ground in the city outskirts into the smoke.
- Wide: the Rust Moth climbing away from the burning city.
**Dialogue:** none.

# ACT 3 — TAKEN

## 21 · SKY WAR
**Setting:** the battle sky (ENV_07). **Who:** no characters (Rust Moth, fighters, strike craft). **Link:** CUT.
- Wide: white-gold Solace fighters and black Khorr strike craft crossing fire.
- The Rust Moth flies through the middle of it, always left to right.
- Strike craft break off and turn after it.
- A Solace fighter streaks in from above and blasts the lead strike craft.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 22 · RHEA
**Setting:** Rhea's fighter cockpit, battle sky outside (ENV_07). **Who:** Rhea. **Link:** CUT.
- Rhea flies with one hand, scanning the sky.
- She sees the Rust Moth with strike craft behind it.
- She pushes her throttle forward and banks toward them.
- Her fighter pulls alongside the Rust Moth, then turns into the strike craft.
**Dialogue:** Rhea (radio, calm) "Freighter — get clear. I'll draw them off."

## 23 · HIT
**Setting:** the Rust Moth cockpit (ENV_04) and exterior (ENV_09). **Who:** Kael, Nyra, Teo. **Link:** C.
- Exterior: above them the warship turns; a red beam fires.
- The beam grazes the Rust Moth's left engine; it bursts into flame.
- Cockpit: alarms, flashing amber lights; Kael fights the controls; Nyra and Teo brace.
- Exterior: the ship levels off, trailing smoke, dropping toward the wastes.
**Dialogue:** Kael "Hold on to something!"

## 24 · DOWN
**Setting:** the salt flats (ENV_10) — the Rust Moth (ENV_09). **Who:** no characters visible. **Link:** C.
- The Rust Moth glides low over the white salt flat, engine smoking.
- Its belly hits the salt; crust bursts up in a white spray.
- It skids for hundreds of metres, panels tearing off, a white cloud rolling behind.
- It stops, tilted on one wing, in the settling salt.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 25 · STILL BREATHING
**Setting:** beside the wreck on the salt flat (ENV_10). **Who:** Kael, Nyra, Teo, Dax. **Link:** C.
- The side hatch bangs open; Dax climbs out coughing.
- Kael helps Teo down; Nyra drops from the wing.
- The four stand in the silence, squinting at the endless white under the twin suns.
- Dax looks at the ship and shakes his head.
**Dialogue:** Kael "Everyone in one piece?" Dax "She's not flying today."

## 26 · FIRELIGHT
**Setting:** the salt flat at night (ENV_10), the gas giant huge in the sky. **Who:** Teo, Kael. **Link:** CUT.
- A small fire beside the wreck. Teo sits staring at his gauntlet.
- Kael sits down next to him and holds out his alien arm; the amber lines glow softly.
- The two glows side by side, pulsing slowly together.
- Teo looks at Kael; Kael gives a small nod. Embers drift up.
**Dialogue:** Teo "Does it hurt?" Kael "Every day." … "You get used to it."

## 27 · RED ON THE HORIZON
**Setting:** the salt flat at dawn (ENV_10). **Who:** Nyra, Hunters. **Link:** CUT.
- Nyra wakes by the cold fire, hand going to her sword.
- Far across the salt, a line of red lights moves toward the wreck.
- Closer: Hunters running in a wide line, red scanner beams sweeping the ground.
- Nyra shakes Kael awake; he is on his feet instantly.
**Dialogue:** Nyra (whisper) "They found us."

## 28 · THE COMMANDER  🔴
**Setting:** the salt flat (ENV_10). **Who:** Commander, Nyra, Kael (Hunters in background). **Link:** C.
- A dropship lands behind the Hunters' line; the Commander steps down, greatsword in hand.
- Nyra charges him; he swings the greatsword into the ground — a red shockwave throws Nyra and Kael back across the salt.
- They land hard and try to rise; the Hunters close in around them.
- The Commander turns his vertical red eye toward the wreck.
**Dialogue:** Commander "Enough."

## 29 · THE CHOICE
**Setting:** the salt flat (ENV_10). **Who:** Teo, Commander. **Link:** C.
- Teo steps out from behind the wreck.
- He looks at Nyra and Kael on the ground, then at the Commander.
- He lowers his gauntlet and walks across the salt toward the Commander by himself.
- He stops in front of him and looks up, steady.
**Dialogue:** Teo "Let them go. I'll come."

## 30 · TAKEN
**Setting:** the salt flat (ENV_10), the warship far above (ENV_05b). **Who:** Kael, Nyra. **Link:** C.
- Teo walks up the dropship ramp beside the Commander. He does not look back.
- Nyra gets up and runs toward it; Kael catches her arm and holds her back.
- The dropship lifts off, blowing salt across them.
- It climbs toward the distant warship. Nyra and Kael watch it go.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "Let me go!" Kael "Not like this."

## 31 · PROMISE
**Setting:** the wreck on the salt flat (ENV_10), afternoon. **Who:** Kael, Nyra, Dax. **Link:** CUT.
- Dax welds the burnt engine, sparks flying.
- Kael and Nyra lift a hull panel into place together.
- Nyra looks up at the warship, a dark shape in the sky.
- Kael follows her look and wipes his hands.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "We get him back." Kael "We get him back."

# ACT 4 — DAWN OVER VEYRA

## 32 · THE BELLY
**Setting:** the warship's great hall (ENV_05a). **Who:** Teo, two soldiers. **Link:** CUT.
- A massive obsidian hall, red light running along the floor channels.
- Teo walks between two soldiers, head up, not restrained.
- He passes rows of silent soldiers standing in formation.
- A huge door opens ahead of him into red light.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 33 · INFILTRATION
**Setting:** outside the warship (ENV_05b). **Who:** no characters visible (Rust Moth, ENV_09). **Link:** CUT.
- The patched Rust Moth climbs toward the warship's belly, flying through the exhaust haze.
- It slides under the huge red vents, dwarfed by the hull.
- Clamps shoot out and lock onto an exhaust grate.
- The engines cut. It hangs there, unseen.
**Dialogue:** Dax (radio) "We're on."

## 34 · INSIDE
**Setting:** the warship's great hall (ENV_05a), a side walkway. **Who:** Kael, Nyra, Dax. **Link:** CUT.
- A vent panel pops open; Kael, Nyra and Dax drop onto a narrow walkway.
- They move quietly, left to right, keeping low.
- A patrol passes below; they freeze in the shadows.
- They reach a doorway glowing amber at the far end — the gauntlet's light.
**Dialogue:** Kael (whisper) "That's him."

## 35 · THE CORE
**Setting:** the warship's bridge (ENV_05c) around a red core. **Who:** Teo, Commander. **Link:** CUT.
- Teo stands before a pulsing red core; the Commander holds his gauntlet arm toward it.
- Red energy pulls at the gauntlet; amber light fights back up Teo's arm.
- Teo grits his teeth; the amber pushes the red back.
- The Commander grips harder; the whole bridge trembles.
**Dialogue:** Commander "It was never yours." Teo "It chose me."

## 36 · BREAKTHROUGH  🔴
**Setting:** the bridge (ENV_05c). **Who:** Nyra, one Hunter. **Link:** C.
- The bridge door blasts open; Nyra runs in, swords igniting.
- The last Hunter blocks her path. One-on-one duel on the tiered platform.
- The Hunter drives her to the edge; she spins free.
- She knocks its glaive away and kicks it off the platform into the dark.
**Dialogue:** Nyra "Out of my way."

## 37 · TWO LIGHTS
**Setting:** the bridge (ENV_05c). **Who:** Teo, Kael, Commander. **Link:** C.
- Kael reaches Teo and grabs his gauntlet hand with his alien arm.
- Amber light races up both their arms and joins in one blinding glow.
- A combined amber blast throws the Commander backward into the red core.
- The core cracks; red light pours out; alarms everywhere.
**Dialogue:** Kael "Together." Teo "Together."

## 38 · THE FALL OF KHORR
**Setting:** the sky (ENV_06) and the warship (ENV_05b). **Who:** no characters. **Link:** CUT.
- Explosions ripple along the warship's hull from the inside.
- Its red vents flicker and go dark one by one.
- The giant ship breaks in two and starts to fall slowly toward the clouds.
- Wide: burning halves sinking under the ringed gas giant.
**Dialogue:** none.

## 39 · ESCAPE
**Setting:** the Rust Moth cockpit (ENV_04) and exterior (ENV_09). **Who:** Kael, Teo, Nyra, Dax. **Link:** CUT.
- The Rust Moth drops free of the falling warship and dives away, left to right.
- Cockpit: Kael pushes the throttle; Nyra, Teo and Dax brace.
- Debris falls past the glass; they fly clear.
- Exterior: the Rust Moth flies out into clean golden sky.
**Dialogue:** Dax "Tell me we're clear." Kael "We're clear."

## 40 · DAWN
**Setting:** the ruins of Solace at dawn (ENV_02b / ENV_01). **Who:** Teo, Nyra, Kael, Dax, Rhea, Oris. **Link:** CUT.
- The Rust Moth lands among the ruined bone-white towers as the twin suns rise.
- The four step down the ramp. Rhea and Oris walk out of the smoke to meet them.
- Teo looks at his gauntlet; its glow softens and goes quiet.
- Wide: the six of them standing in the ruins, the Spine Tower still standing, light spreading across Solace.
**Dialogue:** Oris "The city will rise again." Teo "So will we."
