=== CLIP 1b - THREE PAIRS SEARCHING IN 3 DIFFERENT PLACES (Kling 3.0 Omni via Firefly) ===
Comes right after the split (P7_CLIP1_split_EDIT). I cut it 2 ways: 3 cuts one after another, AND a 3-panel split-screen.
SETTINGS: 15 s | 16:9 | Multi-shot ON (3 shots) | No first frame
CHIPS: @warriorwithspear

----- PASTE BELOW -----
Night, deep dense wet tropical rainforest, cold blue moonlight through the thick canopy, humid mist. Tribal hunters from @warriorwithspear: carved wooden masks with feathers, palm-fibre capes, white dot body paint, long wooden spears. No torches. Each shot shows ONLY TWO hunters, in a different part of the jungle.

SHOT 1 (0-5 s): Two hunters push slowly through very thick tall bushes and giant wet ferns that reach their chests. The first one parts the leaves with his spear, the second one follows close behind, watching the sides. Medium shot from the side, the camera moves slowly with them. Leaves swing back behind them.

SHOT 2 (5-10 s): Two different hunters walk carefully along a narrow jungle stream, stepping from one mossy rock to the next, water flowing around their feet. One crouches and looks at the wet mud on the bank, the other stands guard with his spear raised, looking into the dark trees. Wide shot, the camera stays still.

SHOT 3 (10-15 s): Two other hunters climb between huge twisted tree roots and big mossy boulders, going up a steep slope toward rocky hills. High above them a thin line of grey smoke rises into the moonlight. They stop, look up at it, and keep climbing, low and silent. Low shot from behind.

Only two hunters in each shot, nobody else. Same masks and costumes in every shot. Deep green wet rainforest, no dry ground. No talking. No torches, no text, no watermark.
Sound: night insects, rustling leaves, flowing water in shot 2, quiet footsteps, heavy breathing through masks. No music, no English words.
