=== CLIP 2 - THE SCENT LEADS THEM TO THE CAVE (Kling 3.0 Omni via Firefly) ===
No smoke. One quick smell moment, no dog-like sniffing. Night all the way.
SETTINGS: 15 s | 16:9 | Multi-shot ON (3 shots) | No first frame
CHIPS: @warriorwithspear  @caveenterance

----- PASTE BELOW -----
Night, deep wet rainforest at the foot of rocky hills, cold blue moonlight, light mist. Two tribal hunters from @warriorwithspear: carved wooden masks with feathers, palm-fibre capes, white dot body paint, long wooden spears. No torches.

SHOT 1 (0-4 s): Medium shot. The two hunters walk slowly between mossy rocks and ferns. A soft breeze moves the leaves. The front hunter suddenly stops and turns his masked head toward the wind for one second, as if he caught a scent. He lifts two fingers and points ahead. Both move on quickly and quietly.

SHOT 2 (4-9 s): The two hunters reach a big rock wall covered in roots and vines. They press their backs against the rock and slide along it. The front hunter slowly leans his head around the corner of the rock and sees the dark mouth of the cave from @caveenterance, quiet, faint moonlight on the entrance. He signals with his hand: "in there".

SHOT 3 (9-15 s): Low shot from inside the dark cave looking out at the moonlit entrance. The two hunters step inside one after the other, spears pointed forward, crouching low, moving very slowly into the darkness. Their shapes fade into the black as they come closer to the camera.

Only these two hunters, nobody else. Same masks and costumes in every shot. Night, no daylight, no smoke, no torches, no text, no watermark.
Sound: night insects, soft wind, quiet footsteps on wet stone, slow breathing through the masks, then deep silence inside the cave. No music, no English words.
----- END -----
