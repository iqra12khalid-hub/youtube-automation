NIGHT. One continuous shot, camera still, same position as the first frame, inside the dark cave from @caveinside looking toward the entrance. The scene stays here the whole time: the rocky cave walls and the moonlit entrance are always visible in the background, never a black empty void, no other place, no other people.

LIGHT: dark, but the bodies are clearly readable. Cold blue moonlight comes in from the entrance behind them and draws a bright blue rim of light along the edges of every body. Faces stay in shadow, but the shape and details of each body are clearly visible.

THE TWO WARRIORS, exactly like @warriorwithspear: tall muscular tribal men, carved wooden masks with feather crests on top, palm-fibre capes on the shoulders, white dot body paint, woven waist cloths, long wooden spears. The moonlight outlines their feathers, capes and spears.

THE CREATURE, exactly like @Monster: thin bony grey body, bald round head, large pointed ears, one big round eye in the middle of its face that catches a small glint of moonlight, a necklace of round discs, ragged cloth around the waist, long thin arms and fingers. Only a little taller than the warriors. The moonlight outlines its bald head, ears and thin limbs.

ACTION:
0-3 s: The two warriors walk slowly into the cave, spears pointed forward.
3-5 s: The creature runs at them out of the dark inside of the cave, from the front, near the camera. It never comes from the entrance or from outside.
5-10 s: A short rough struggle: the creature knocks the first warrior down; the second warrior's spear breaks against the creature's leg and a short broken piece stays in it; the creature shoves him down too. Both warriors lie on the ground, dazed.
10-15 s: The creature stands over them, bends, grips the broken spear piece in its leg, pulls it free and throws its head back with a long, huge roar of pain that echoes out of the cave.

All three stay in the frame the whole time, nobody vanishes, nobody poses for the camera. No blood, no torches, no daylight, no text, no watermark.
Sound: footsteps, a rush, wood snapping, bodies hitting stone, heavy breathing, a long deep echoing roar. No music, no English words.
