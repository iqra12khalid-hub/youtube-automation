NIGHT in every shot. Pitch dark, cold blue moonlight only, no daylight. One single long roar runs through the whole video without stopping: it starts in shot 1 and is still echoing, a little fainter, in shots 2 and 3.

SHOT 1 (0-4 s): Same shot as the first frame. The tall dark silhouette of @Monster stands in the moonlit cave mouth. It clenches its fists, throws its head back and lets out a huge, deep, long roar. Its whole body shakes. It stays a silhouette, face not lit.

SHOT 2 (4-9 s): Far away, deep in a dense wet rainforest at night: giant mossy trees, hanging vines, tall ferns, mist. Two tribal hunters from @warriorwithspear, carved wooden masks with feathers, palm-fibre capes, long spears, are creeping through the ferns. The roar echoes through the trees. They freeze and snap their heads toward the sound. One points with his spear, and both start running fast through the ferns toward it.

SHOT 3 (9-15 s): A completely different place in the deep jungle at night: a narrow rocky stream between huge twisted roots, mist over the water. Two other hunters from @warriorwithspear hear the same roar still echoing. They look up, grip their spears and run, splashing through the stream and leaping over the roots, into the dark trees. The camera follows them from behind.

Same masks and costumes on all four hunters. Deep wet green rainforest, cold blue moonlight, no daylight, no torches, no cave in shots 2 and 3, no text, no watermark.
Sound: one continuous huge deep roar echoing from shot 1 through shot 3, birds flapping away, insects falling silent, then fast footsteps, splashing water, heavy breathing. No music, no English words.
