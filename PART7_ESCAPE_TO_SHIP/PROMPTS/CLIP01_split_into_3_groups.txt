SHOT 1 (0-5 s): Night in a dark jungle, cold blue moonlight, thin mist between the trees. Six tribal hunters from @warriorwithspear, carved wooden masks with feathers, palm-fibre capes, white dot body paint, long wooden spears, push slowly through the ferns, searching the ground and the bushes. No torches. They stop in a small clearing.

SHOT 2 (5-10 s): The leader kneels and draws three lines in the dirt with the tip of his spear, then points left, straight ahead, and right. He speaks low and fast in an invented tribal language: "TOKA! MAHU! RAN-TI!" The others nod.

SHOT 3 (10-15 s): Wide shot of the clearing. The six hunters split into three pairs and move off silently in three different directions into the dark jungle. One pair walks straight toward the rocky hills, where a very thin line of grey smoke rises in the moonlight. The camera stays still as they disappear between the trees.

Same masks and costumes on every hunter, six hunters only, nobody else. No torches, no text, no watermark.
Sound: night insects, soft wind, rustling ferns, a low whispered command, quiet footsteps, then silence. No music, no English words.
