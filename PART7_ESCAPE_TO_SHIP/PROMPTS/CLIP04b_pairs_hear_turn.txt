=== CLIP 4b - BOTH PAIRS HEAR THE SCREAM, STOP AND TURN (for a 2-panel split screen) ===
Kling makes the two pairs as 2 shots; I put them side by side (left = pair 1, right = pair 2) with the monster's scream over both.
Far from the cave: deep jungle only.
SETTINGS: 15 s | 16:9 | Multi-shot ON (2 shots) | No first frame
CHIPS: @warriorwithspear

----- PASTE BELOW -----
NIGHT. Pitch dark deep rainforest, cold blue moonlight through the canopy, mist. No daylight, no cave, no rocks or cliffs, only deep jungle. In both shots the two hunters stay in the CENTER of the frame, medium-wide shot, camera still.

Hunters from @warriorwithspear: carved wooden masks with feather crests, palm-fibre capes, white dot body paint, woven waist cloths, long wooden spears.

SHOT 1 (0-7 s): Two hunters walk quickly AWAY from the camera through tall wet ferns between giant mossy trees, their backs to us. Suddenly a huge distant scream echoes through the jungle from behind them. Both stop dead in their tracks. They slowly turn around to face the camera, the direction of the scream, spears raised, heads tilted, listening. Then they start running back toward the camera.

SHOT 2 (7-15 s): A different place in the deep jungle: a shallow rocky stream between huge twisted roots. Two other hunters wade AWAY from the camera through the water, their backs to us. The same distant scream echoes. Both stop, water around their legs, and turn around to face the camera, the direction of the scream, spears raised, listening. Then they start running back toward the camera, splashing.

Same masks and costumes on all four hunters. Pitch dark night, no daylight, no torches, no cave, no text, no watermark.
Sound: jungle night insects, then a huge distant echoing scream, sudden silence, heavy breathing, then running footsteps and splashing. No music, no English words.
----- END -----
