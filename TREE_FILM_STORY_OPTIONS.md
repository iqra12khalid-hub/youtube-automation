# Our own tree film - story options (draft, not approved yet)

Goal: same message as Forevergreen (trees keep us alive, save them, plant them) but our OWN story, characters, setting and look, so it is safe for YouTube (no copyright claim) and feels new.

Different from Forevergreen on purpose:
- No bear, no fir tree, no snow, no campfire, no "tree dies to save the animal".
- New setting, new danger, new ending.
- Same craft: no dialogue, music + sound + faces, short fast shots in danger, long slow shots in emotion.

---

## Option A (recommended): "ROOTS"  ~8-9 min
**Setting:** a green hill above a river village (South Asian feel: banyan tree, monsoon, mud houses).
**Characters:**
- **Bargad** - a huge old banyan tree with a kind face in its bark, hanging roots like a beard.
- **Chiku** - a small, cheeky squirrel who lives in Bargad's trunk.
- **The Woodcutter** - a grumpy (comic, not evil) man with an axe and a donkey cart.

**Story:**
1. **Home (2 min)** - Morning. Chiku wakes inside Bargad. Birds, monkeys and a mother goat rest in its shade. Bargad gives shade, fruit, a swing of roots. Pure joy.
2. **The axe (2 min)** - The woodcutter comes up the hill. Tree by tree he cuts the hillside bare and carts the wood away. Chiku throws figs at him (comedy). Bargad is too big, he marks it with a red X: "tomorrow".
3. **Heat (1 min)** - The bare hill bakes in the sun. Animals crowd under Bargad, the only shade left. The woodcutter, tired and thirsty, also sits in its shade, and falls asleep against it.
4. **The storm (2 min, fast cuts)** - Monsoon night. The bare, rootless hillside turns into a mudslide, sliding toward the village and the woodcutter's hut. Bargad's roots grip the earth; it holds the slope above the village. Chiku is washed out of the trunk and Bargad's hanging roots catch him. The woodcutter wakes in the storm, clinging to the same roots.
5. **Morning (1 min, slow)** - Everything around is brown mud. Only Bargad's hill still stands, and the village below is safe. The woodcutter looks up at the tree, then at his axe.
6. **Plant (1 min)** - He drops the axe. He and Chiku plant Bargad's seeds/cuttings on the bare hill. Time-lapse: the hill turns green again. Last shot: the red X washed off the bark, Bargad smiles.

**Message:** trees hold the earth, give shade, food and homes, and save us from floods. Cut them and we lose all of it. Plant them back.
**Why it works for AI:** few locations (hill, tree, village), few characters, animals and nature = AI's strongest area, one big spectacle (storm/mudslide) with no complex fighting.

## Option B: "THE LAST TREE"  ~6-7 min
A dry desert town where only one tree is left. A little camel calf and a hoopoe bird depend on it. Everyone is too busy to care, until a sandstorm hits and only the area around the tree survives. Ends with the town planting a green belt. (Message: shade, cooling, stopping sand and heat.)

## Option C: "BREATH"  ~5 min, simpler
A smoggy city. A small potted plant on a balcony is the only green thing. A sparrow finds it. The film shows what one tree does (clean air, birds, cool shade) by growing one tree into a full park over time.
