CLIP 5 - THE SHIP SEES THE SMOKE (Kling, multi-shot)

Daytime in every shot: bright soft daylight, light sea mist, deep blue ocean. The same tropical island in every shot: white sand beach, turquoise water, a thick wall of green jungle behind the beach.
SHOT 1 (0-4 s): Wide shot on the open ocean: an old wooden sailing ship with weathered white sails glides slowly across the calm sea, waves breaking at its bow, sea birds flying. Far on the horizon, a small green island.
SHOT 2 (4-7 s): On the deck, a bearded sailor in a faded shirt leans on the wooden rail and looks through an old brass spyglass toward the island. Wind in his hair.
SHOT 3 (7-11 s): Spyglass view (round black edge): the island's white beach, and on the sand a small burnt fire pit of driftwood still smouldering, a thin column of grey smoke rising straight up into the sky. Nobody on the beach.
SHOT 4 (11-15 s): The sailor lowers the spyglass, eyes wide, turns and shouts to the crew behind him: "Smoke! There's someone on that island!" He points at the island. Two other sailors rush to the rail to look.
Same daylight in every shot. No tribe, no warriors, no creature, no modern boats, no text.
