# THE ONE-EYED ISLAND — PART 1 RUN SHEET (fast mode)

From the beach exit (B3) to the Part 1 cliffhanger. Everything is decided. Just go down the list.

## How to work fast
1. **Right now:** start all **B-roll runs** (no frame, no elements, text only). Use several tabs and don't wait for each one.
2. **While they render:** make **all 7 frames** in one image session (same tool you used for B1/B2/B3).
3. Send me **all frames together** in one message → I check them in one go.
4. Then do the **frame runs** (start frame = that frame, no elements, 5s, Audio ON).
5. Send results **in batches**. Only redo a shot if the face/place is clearly wrong. Small things → fix in the edit.

Settings for every video run: **Multi-shot OFF · 5s · Audio ON · no elements**.

## BATCH A — B-ROLL (text only, start now, no frame)

**A1 · Hands gathering sticks**
```
Close-up of a young woman's hands with a white t-shirt sleeve picking up dry sticks from the ground at the edge of a tropical jungle, snapping one stick in half, tucking a dry brown coconut husk under her arm. Warm late afternoon light, fallen palm leaves, sand. No face visible. Realistic, natural motion. Sound: sticks snapping, dry leaves rustling, distant waves.
```

**A2 · Sunset on the sea**
```
Wide shot of a calm tropical sea at sunset, the orange sun touching the horizon, the sky turning orange and pink, gentle waves rolling onto white sand in the foreground, a few palm leaves moving in the breeze at the edge of frame. Slow, peaceful, realistic, no people. Sound: soft waves, light wind, a few distant seabirds.
```

**A3 · Hands spinning the fire stick**
```
Close-up of a young woman's hands spinning a straight wooden stick fast between her flat palms, the tip drilling into a notch in a flat piece of dry wood on the sand, black wood dust gathering, a thin wisp of smoke starting. Dusk light, blue-purple. No face visible. Realistic, real-time speed. Sound: fast wood rubbing, heavy breathing.
```

**A4 · The ember**
```
Extreme macro close-up of black wood dust in a notch of dry wood, a tiny orange ember starts to glow and pulse, thick smoke curling up, fingers carefully lift the glowing dust onto a green leaf. Nearly dark, the ember is the only warm light. Realistic. Sound: faint crackle, a soft gasp.
```

**A5 · Fire catches**
```
Close-up low angle: a young woman's hands push a smoking ball of coconut fibre under a small cone of dry sticks and leaves on the sand; the leaves catch fire, flames climb the sticks and the fire grows bright. Night, warm orange light on her hands. No face visible. Realistic, real-time. Sound: fire crackling and growing, soft breathing.
```

**A6 · Tiny fire on the night beach**
```
Very wide static shot at night from the sea side: a small warm campfire glows at the top of a dark empty beach under a giant tree, the black jungle behind it, a small seated figure next to the fire hugging her knees, tiny in frame. A sky full of stars above, calm dark sea in the foreground. Realistic, cinematic, lonely. Sound: soft waves, fire crackle, night insects.
```

**A7 · THE EYE (cliffhanger)**
```
Night, dark dense jungle edge, warm orange firelight flickering on the big leaves at the front. Deep in the black between the trees, one single huge eye slowly opens, wet and glossy, reflecting the firelight, it does not blink. Nothing else of the creature is visible. Camera stays still, very slow push in. Realistic, dark, suspense, not fantasy. Sound: fire crackle far away, insects suddenly go silent, one deep slow breath.
```

**A8 · Watcher's view (end shot)**
```
Night, point of view from deep inside the dark jungle looking out through big black leaves: far away on the beach a small campfire glows and a small figure sits next to it. Slowly, large leaves close in front of the view until it goes completely black. Realistic, dark, suspense. Sound: slow heavy breathing very close, leaves rustling.
```
