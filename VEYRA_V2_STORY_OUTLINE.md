# VEYRA — Clean Workflow v2: Story Outline

**Length:** ~20 minutes · **40 clips × 30 s** · Seedance 2.5 on Dola
**World & characters:** kept from the original VEYRA bible. **Enemies: only two types — Khorr Soldiers and the Commander** (no Hunters/Elite). **Enemy ships: only the mothership and its small ships** (no dropship) — soldiers travel down red beams from the mothership. **Teo is redesigned as a young adult** (new `CHAR_Teo` sheet, same outfit and gauntlet). No child characters in the film.

---

## Writing rules for this script (built from everything we tested)

1. **One clear action per shot**, 8 short shots per clip.
2. **Max 2–3 characters doing things at once.** Crowds only as background reaction.
3. **Chases are short, wide and one-directional.** No fights on moving vehicles.
4. **Fights are one-on-one**, in medium / close shots.
5. **Big spectacle with no people** (warship, launches, explosions) — AI's strongest area.
6. **Tension instead of constant action** — scanners, red eyes in steam, footsteps above.
7. **Few locations, reused often**, each with a location sheet + SET LOCK + LIGHT LOCK.
8. **Only 4 high-complexity clips** in the whole film (marked 🔴).
9. **Teo is never the direct target of a strike** — enemies go after Nyra / Kael / Dax; Teo acts (gauntlet) or is protected.
10. **No gore, no graphic harm.** Hits knock people back; nobody is shown wounded in detail.
