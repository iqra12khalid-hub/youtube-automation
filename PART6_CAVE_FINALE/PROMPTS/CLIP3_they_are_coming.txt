SHOT 2 (4-10 s): Medium shot inside the same cave as @caveinside, roots hanging from the rock ceiling, a small fire burning low on the ground. @Monster, with its single large eye in the middle of its forehead and its necklace, is already crouching awake, its head turned toward the dark cave entrance. It gently pulls Noor behind a large rock against the cave wall and crouches in front of her like a guard.

SHOT 3 (10-15 s): Low dark shot from inside the cave looking toward the entrance, blue moonlight outside. One by one, the shiny tips of long wooden spears slowly appear around the edge of the entrance, then the shadows of feathered masks. In the darkness close to the camera, the monster's single large eye opens and glows softly. Hold on the eye.

CONTINUITY: same cave in every shot, only Noor and the monster inside; the tribe members are only shadows and spear tips at the entrance. Noor keeps the same face, clothes and face paint. No jumps, no new people inside the cave.
Sound: faint tribal chant outside, the fire hissing out, then near silence, slow breathing, soft footsteps on stone, a pebble rolling. No music, no English words.
