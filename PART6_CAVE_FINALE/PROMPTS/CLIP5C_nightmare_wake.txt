SHOT 1 (0-7 s): Same wide shot as the first frame, the camera stays still. @Noora3 lies asleep by the small fire and @Monster with its one big eye sits beside her, very still. Out of the darkness at the back of the cave, the old woman from @tribeoldwomen slowly appears, and behind her the tall chief with his feathered crown and staff stands as a dark shape. The old woman's eyes lock on Noor. Suddenly she rushes forward across the cave, kneels over Noor and reaches both hands down toward Noor's neck. At the very moment her fingers touch, hard cut.

SHOT 2 (7-11 s): Close-up. Noor's eyes snap open and she sits up very fast with a loud gasp, like waking from a nightmare, breathing hard, both hands on her own neck, eyes wide with fear, looking around the cave. Nobody is there. The fire is still burning warm. She does not smile.

SHOT 3 (11-15 s): Medium shot. @Monster with its one big eye leans toward her and gently puts one hand on her shoulder. Noor slowly lowers her hands, still breathing fast, and wipes her face. Then, very far away outside, a faint tribal chant: "HOO-HA! HU!" The monster's head turns sharply toward the dark cave entrance. Noor freezes.

Same cave and faces in every shot, warm firelight. No text, no watermark.
Sound: quiet fire, a low rising hum as the old woman comes closer, a sharp gasp, fast breathing, then a faint distant chant. No music, no English words.
