# Forevergreen - analysis (13:05 incl. ~1:50 credits, 3D animated, no dialogue)

Reference only. We do NOT copy its characters, plot or look. We copy the *craft*: how it tells a "save the trees" message with no words.

## Story (by section)
| Time | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:18 | Title | Black screen with title text |
| 0:18-3:00 | Winter | Orphaned bear cub alone in the snow at night. An old fir tree (face in the trunk) secretly shelters it, gives it a pinecone to eat. Spring: the tree raises the cub (porcupine friend, berries). A tiny sprout appears. |
| 3:00-5:30 | Growing up | Cub grows into a big bear. Finds a chip bag (human junk food) and gets hooked. Tree tries to warn him with its branches; bear ignores it. |
| 5:30-7:00 | Campsite | Bear goes down the waterfall to a human campsite (signpost, picnic table, trash). Eats junk, falls asleep in the litter. |
| 7:00-9:20 | Fire | Night: the campfire spreads into a forest fire. Bear runs back up. The burning tree bends down, lifts him to safety, and falls. Black screen. |
| 9:20-10:30 | Ash | Grey burned land, smoke. Bear alone and sad. A green seedling grows from the tree's last pinecone. |
| 10:30-11:45 | New forest | Bear plants pinecones. Years pass: a new forest, bear with his own cub. |
| 11:45-13:05 | Credits | Illustrated credits |

Structure: 3 min warm bond -> 2.5 min temptation/mistake -> 2.5 min disaster + sacrifice -> 1 min grief -> 1 min hope.

## What it does well (copy these rules)
1. The message is never said. It is shown: shelter, food, a home, then the loss of all of it.
2. The tree is a character: eyes and a mouth in the bark, emotion through small branch movements.
3. Humans are never shown, only their trash and their fire. The danger is the *result* of people.
4. Colour = mood: cold blue (alone), warm green/gold (tree cares), orange/red (fire), grey (loss), sunrise (hope).
5. The fire is fast cuts (1.8s); the grief and the hope are long, slow shots.
6. Ending = a seed. The message is "plant again", not just "be sad".
